Applications

Where can SLA01 mini linear actuator be used?

SLA01 is recommended for compact push-pull motion, small lifting systems, position adjustment, opening and closing mechanisms, and equipment where quiet and clean electric movement is preferred.

Robotics

Compact push-pull motion for robot arms, service robots, inspection robots, and small automation modules.

Medical Devices

Position adjustment for treatment devices, chairs, beds, rehabilitation equipment, and healthcare mechanisms.

Smart Furniture

Recliners, small lifting cabinets, adjustable desks, hidden compartments, and ergonomic furniture mechanisms.

Automotive Systems

Small hatches, covers, vents, access doors, and special vehicle mechanisms requiring controlled linear movement.
