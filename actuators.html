SLA01 Series Premium Linear Actuator

The SLA01 series is engineered for high-precision and reliable motion control. Featuring a robust aluminum alloy construction and integrated limit switches, it delivers smooth operation across diverse applications from medical beds to heavy-duty industrial automation.

Core Advantages

  • Premium Build: High-grade aluminum alloy outer tube and inner thrust rod.
